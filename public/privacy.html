Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Noisy Mail Generator exists so you don't have to hand your real email address to strangers. It would be strange if we turned around and did the same thing to you. This policy explains exactly what touches our servers and what doesn't.

01What we do not collect

We don't ask for a name, a password, a phone number, or a payment method. There are no accounts. We don't use third-party analytics, advertising pixels, or tracking cookies. If you generate an address and leave, we have nothing that identifies you.

02What we process temporarily

When you generate an address, the address itself and any incoming mail sent to it are stored only while that mailbox is alive. Once a mailbox is recycled by the upstream mail network, its messages are deleted automatically and cannot be recovered by you or by us.

Like almost every service on the internet, our infrastructure logs basic technical request data (such as IP address and user agent) for security and abuse prevention. These logs are rotated and are never sold or shared for marketing purposes.

03Who can see a mailbox

Generated addresses are random, but they are not secret. Anyone who knows or guesses the full address can view its incoming mail while the mailbox is live. Treat every message you receive here as public until the mailbox burns.

04Cookies and local storage

This landing page uses no cookies at all. If you use the copy button, the clipboard action happens entirely inside your browser. Nothing is written to local storage beyond what your browser needs to render the page.

05Your rights

Because we hold no identifying data about you, there is usually nothing to export, correct, or delete. If you believe we hold data connected to you in error, contact us and we will investigate and respond within 30 days.

06Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and surface the change on the landing page before it takes effect.
