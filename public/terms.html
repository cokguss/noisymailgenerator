01The service

Noisy Mail Generator provides free, temporary, receive-only email addresses. Addresses are generated at random, remain active only as long as the upstream mailbox exists, and are destroyed automatically afterward. We make no promise that any specific address stays available or that mail delivery takes less than a few minutes.

02Acceptable use

You agree not to use generated addresses to:

  • circumvent bans, evading enforcement actions on other services;
  • create fraudulent accounts or impersonate other people;
  • receive material you are not legally allowed to possess;
  • abuse sign-up systems at scale, including scripted bulk registration;
  • harass anyone, through replies or through any other channel.

If we detect abuse — unusual volume, fraud signals, or reports from other providers — we may block address ranges, filter domains that refuse temp-mail services, or restrict access entirely.

03No expectation of privacy on shared inboxes

Generated addresses are random but not secret. Anyone who knows the full address can read its incoming mail while the mailbox is alive. Don't send anything to a noisy address you wouldn't leave on a park bench.

04Data loss is a feature

Mailboxes self-destruct. This is intentional. We are not able to restore expired messages, recover lost addresses, or transfer content between addresses. Do not use the service for anything you cannot afford to lose — banking, medical, government, or account recovery email included.
